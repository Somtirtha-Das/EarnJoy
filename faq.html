Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about learning on EarnJoy.

What is EarnJoy?

EarnJoy is a free learning platform designed to make useful courses, lectures and study resources easier to discover and access.

Is EarnJoy free?

Available EarnJoy courses and resources are currently provided for free. Individual third-party platforms linked from EarnJoy may have their own terms.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the currently available public learning resources. Course progress tracking works locally in your browser.

Can I use EarnJoy on mobile?

Yes. EarnJoy is designed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes.

How do I access a course?

Open a course from the homepage, categories page or resources page, then select the available learning material.

Can I suggest a course or report an issue?

Yes. Use the Contact page to share course suggestions, broken resources or content feedback.

Are finance and stock-market materials financial advice?

No. Finance-related content is educational only and should not be treated as personalised investment, trading, tax or financial advice.
