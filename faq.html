01 / FIRST STEPS

Getting started

What is EarnJoy?+

EarnJoy is an open learning platform that brings together structured courses, study materials, lectures and digital books to help people explore knowledge and build skills.

Are EarnJoy courses and resources free?+

The learning resources currently published directly on EarnJoy are available free of charge. External websites and third-party services linked from EarnJoy may have their own terms.

Do I need to create an account?+

No account is currently required to browse the public courses, resources or e-book collections on EarnJoy.

How do I find and start a course?+

Open All Courses from the navigation or homepage, browse or search the catalogue, and select a course to view its overview and available learning materials.

02 / STUDY MATERIALS

Courses & materials

How do I read a course lesson or PDF?+

Open the course page and select Read PDF beside an available lesson. The PDF opens in a new browser tab, where you can read it or use your browser's available download or print options.

Where can I find all available study resources?+

Visit the Resources page to see learning materials grouped by course. You can also open a course directly from the course catalogue.

Can I learn at my own pace?+

Yes. You can work through the available lessons in order or revisit a topic whenever you need to. Follow any suggested sequence shown on the course page.

03 / YOUR LEARNING

Progress & access

Can I track my course progress?+

The current Stock Market course includes lesson-completion checkboxes and a progress indicator. Its progress is stored in the browser on the device you use, not in an online account, and is not synchronised across devices. Clearing browser site data may remove it.

Progress tracking may not be available on every course.

Can I use EarnJoy on a mobile phone or tablet?+

Yes. EarnJoy pages are designed to adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile screens. PDF viewing may also depend on your device and browser.

04 / THE READING ROOM

E-book library

Where can I find EarnJoy e-books?+

Open E-books from the main navigation to explore the published collections. The current featured collection is the C.A.T Success Series, organised around Quantitative Ability, Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Ability.

Are new courses and e-books added?+

EarnJoy is a growing learning library. New courses, topics and e-book collections may be added as they are prepared and published. Check the course catalogue and e-book library for the latest published material.

05 / WE CAN HELP

Help & feedback

What should I do if a PDF or course link does not work?+

Refresh the page and try the link again. If the issue continues, use the Contact page and include the course name, lesson or PDF title, and the page where you found the link. This helps us identify the issue.

Can I suggest a topic or share feedback?+

Yes. Visit the Contact page to share course suggestions, content feedback or a resource issue. You can also visit the official EarnJoy YouTube channel.

06 / A NOTE ON LEARNING

Does EarnJoy provide investment or financial advice?

No. Finance and stock-market materials are for general educational purposes only and are not personalised investment, trading, tax or financial advice. Financial markets involve risk. Read the Terms of Use for more information.
