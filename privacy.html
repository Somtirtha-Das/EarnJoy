01 / THE BASICS

Privacy should be understandable.

EarnJoy is an educational website that publishes courses, learning materials and links to educational content. The public resources can be browsed without creating an EarnJoy account.

This policy applies to the EarnJoy website at earnjoy.das105070.workers.dev and its pages. It describes the website's current implementation as reviewed on the date above. Features and service providers may change over time.

In shortEarnJoy does not currently provide website sign-in, user profiles, payment checkout or a direct website contact form. Some technical information may nevertheless be handled by the infrastructure and external services needed to display the site.
02 / WHAT MAY BE PROCESSED

Information processed when you visit.

Technical and connection information

When a browser requests a website, the hosting and network providers may process technical information such as an IP address, browser and device details, requested page or file, referring page, timestamps and security-related request data. This can be used for delivering pages, troubleshooting, preventing abuse and maintaining service security.

EarnJoy does not operate its own visitor account database or ask you to submit personal details to read its public course materials. We do not control the exact server-log fields or retention periods used by infrastructure providers.

Information you choose to share elsewhere

If you contact EarnJoy through YouTube or another linked platform, the information you provide is handled by that platform under its own policies. Avoid posting passwords, financial details, identity documents or other sensitive information in public comments.

03 / OUTSIDE SERVICES

A few services help the site work.

01

Cloudflare · Website delivery

EarnJoy is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may process connection and technical information to deliver the website, protect its services and maintain security. Its handling of information is governed by its own privacy documentation.

EarnJoy links to its official YouTube channel. Following that link takes you to YouTube; interactions there, including viewing videos, signing in, commenting or subscribing, are subject to Google's and YouTube's terms and privacy practices.

Third-party services may change their technologies and policies independently. EarnJoy does not control their data practices.

04 / COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE

How the site uses browser storage.

The current EarnJoy website code reviewed for this policy does not intentionally set its own analytics, advertising or preference cookies, and does not use browser local storage to build visitor profiles. The course search and filters operate in the page and URL; they are not an account or saved learning-history feature.

However, hosting, security, embedded or linked third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies in their own environments. Your browser may also retain ordinary technical data. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although blocking some technologies may affect third-party features.

05 / PURPOSE & DISCLOSURE

Why information may be used.

Technical information handled by the website's infrastructure may be used to:

  • deliver pages, course files and other requested resources;
  • maintain availability, diagnose errors and improve reliability;
  • detect suspicious traffic, abuse and security incidents; and
  • meet applicable legal requirements or respond to valid legal requests.

EarnJoy does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers involved in hosting, delivery, security or external features, and may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the website and its users.

06 / YOUR CHOICES

You remain in control of your browser.

  • Use browser settings to manage or clear cookies and cached website data.
  • Choose whether to follow external links such as YouTube or Google Fonts requests (blocking external requests may affect appearance or linked features).
  • Do not include sensitive personal information in public comments or messages on third-party platforms.
  • For a privacy question, contact EarnJoy through the official channel listed below.

Privacy rights can depend on your location and the circumstances of processing. Where applicable, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or other action. Requests relating to data held by Cloudflare, Google or YouTube may need to be made directly to those providers.

07 / YOUNGER VISITORS

Educational access and younger users.

EarnJoy is a general educational website and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The site does not currently require visitors to create an account to access public learning resources. Parents or guardians who believe a child has shared personal information through a third-party platform should contact that platform and may also reach out to EarnJoy.

08 / KEEPING THIS CURRENT

When this policy changes.

This policy may be revised when the website adds features, changes providers or when legal or operational requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. Please review it periodically.

09 / QUESTIONS ABOUT PRIVACY

Want to ask us something?

For questions or concerns about this policy or the way EarnJoy operates, visit the official EarnJoy YouTube channel and use the available contact options. Please do not post sensitive personal information publicly.
